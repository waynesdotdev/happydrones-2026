Golden hour is the hour or so after sunrise and before sunset, when the sun sits low and the light turns warm.

## Why it works from above

- **Long shadows** give roofs, trees and gardens depth and shape
- **Warm light** makes brick, stone and grass look richer
- **Softer contrast** means fewer harsh, blown-out areas on roofs and paths

## When midday is fine

Roof checks and construction updates often want flat, even light, because detail matters more than mood. For those, the middle of the day is ideal.
