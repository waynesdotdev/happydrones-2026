If you've been on the Happy Drones website lately, you might have noticed it does more than most drone sites. You can check the weather for your shoot date, build a price in three taps, find the right tour for your type of home, or explore a roof part by part.

People have asked why a one-person drone business bothers. Here's why.

## Because booking a drone shouldn't be guesswork

Most people booking drone work have never done it before. They don't know what it costs, what they'll get, whether the weather will play ball, or which package they need. Usually the answer is "send an email and wait".

I'd rather you could see it for yourself, straight away.

## What's there now

- **[Weather check](/plan/):** pick a date and see the forecast for your area, the best time of day to fly, and a better date if yours looks poor.
- **[Quote builder](/#quote):** a guide price for your property in three taps.
- **[Tour finder](/estate-agents/):** pick a property type and see how the tour runs and which package fits.
- **[Roof explorer](/roof-inspections/):** tap any part of a roof to see what it is and what gets photographed.
- **[Mission planner](/plan/):** build a commercial job, see a guide price and get a mission code.
- **[Example roof report](/report/example/):** see exactly what you get after a roof check.

## Built from your questions

Most of these started as a question someone asked me. "Will it be dark by five?" became the daylight on the weather chart. "Which package does a flat need?" became the tour finder. If you have a question I keep getting asked, the answer probably ends up on the site.

## What's next

I'm working on more: a yearly roof health check, a storm and insurance claim pack, and, once the kit and training are in place, thermal inspections and 3D mapping. You can [see what's coming soon](/services/) and register your interest in anything that isn't ready yet.

## Tell me what to build

If there's something that would make booking or using drone work easier for you, tell me. Message me, comment on a post, or use the [contact form](/#contact). The best ideas so far have come from customers.
