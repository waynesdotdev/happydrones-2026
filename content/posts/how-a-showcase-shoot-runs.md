A Showcase package covers listing photos, aerial shots and a walkthrough video. Here's how it all fits into one visit of about two hours.

## 1. Walk the property

I start with a quick walk round with the vendor or agent: which rooms matter most, which doors stay open, where pets and people will be.

## 2. Exteriors and aerial first

While the light is right outside, I shoot the front and rear exteriors, then fly the Mini 4 Pro for the plot, garden and street views. It also gives the vendor time to finish the last bits of tidying indoors.

## 3. Interior photos

Room by room with the mirrorless camera on a tripod, taking several exposures of each view so both the room and the view out of the window look right.

## 4. The walkthrough

Last, a smooth walk through the home on the Osmo Pocket 3, in the order a buyer would see it on a viewing. I finish with a vertical cut for social media.

## 5. Delivery

Within 48–72 hours, the agent or vendor gets one download link: photos sized for the portals, the walkthrough video, and the social cut, all named by address.
