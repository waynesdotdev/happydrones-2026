If your agency lists four or more homes a month, paying a deposit for every shoot and chasing an invoice per listing makes no sense. That's what agent accounts are for.

## What you get

- **10% off every package**
- **A regular weekly shoot slot**, so listings don't wait
- **No deposit per listing**
- **Files as soon as they're ready**, without waiting for payment per listing
- **One invoice at the end of the month**, with every address listed, paid within 30 days
- **White label at £15 a listing** instead of £29

## How it works

1. **Apply** using the [account form](/estate-agents/#account). I reply within a day with the agreement to sign once. It covers every listing on the account.
2. **Send addresses** by WhatsApp, email or phone. I confirm the address, package and date in writing.
3. **Files straight away**, usually within 48–72 hours of the shoot.
4. **One invoice a month**, itemised by address, at 10% off.

## The credit limit

Accounts have a £1,000 credit limit: the most that can be owed at once, counting shoots done but not yet paid for. Most agencies never get close, because each month's invoice is paid within 30 days. If an invoice goes overdue, bookings pause until it's paid. The full terms are in clause 3.9 of the [booking agreement](/terms/).

## Purchase orders

If your agency needs a purchase order number on invoices, say so on the form and I'll include it on every invoice.

## Not four a month yet?

Pay as you go works for any number of listings, with a 25% deposit per shoot. Or buy a bundle of 10 listings up front for the best price per listing.
