A walkthrough video is the next best thing to a viewing. A one-take walkthrough goes a step further: it's one smooth, continuous move through the home, so buyers follow the route as if they were walking it.

## How it's filmed

I use a handheld stabilised camera that glides at walking height. Starting at the front door, the tour moves through the hall, the main rooms and out into the garden, slowly and steadily, without cuts. Then a few aerial shots from the drone show the house and its setting.

On the [walkthrough page](/walkthrough-video/) you can scroll through an example tour: the page itself becomes the playhead.

## Why buyers like it

- **They understand the layout.** Photos show rooms. A walkthrough shows how they connect.
- **No surprises at the viewing.** People who book a viewing after watching it already like the house.
- **It's watchable on a phone,** which is where most people look at listings.

## Why agents like it

- **Add it as the listing's video tour**
- **One shoot, two shapes:** a landscape version for portals and websites, and a vertical cut for social media
- **Your branding:** agency-branded videos with your logo on the opening and end cards are included, or fully white label if you prefer

## What makes a good walkthrough

- A tidy home, every light on (see my [preparation checklist](/journal/get-your-home-ready-for-photos/))
- A planned route, so the tour flows naturally
- Slow, smooth movement: nothing sudden, no dizzy spins
