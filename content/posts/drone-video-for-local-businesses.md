Running a local business, you know you should post on social media more. You also know you haven't got time. One morning of filming can fill a month.

## How one morning becomes a month

On a typical shoot I capture:

- **Aerials** of your premises and the area around it: the high street, the coast, the village
- **Close-up video** of what you do: the coffee being made, the van being loaded, the work in progress
- **Your team**, if they're happy to be filmed
- **Vertical clips** cut for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok

That gives you a mix of photos and short clips you can post two or three times a week for a month or more. You can see how it fills a calendar on the [local business page](/local-business/), where one morning's filming turns into a month of posts.

## Who it suits

- Cafés, restaurants and pubs
- Shops and salons
- Trades: builders, landscapers, roofers
- Gyms, venues and attractions

## What makes it work

- **A plan before the day.** We agree the shots and the times, like the lunch rush or the van leaving in the morning.
- **Real moments.** People respond to the real thing, not stock footage.
- **The right shapes.** Vertical for social, landscape for your website and Google profile.

## Your Google profile too

Fresh photos and a short video on your Google Business Profile help people find you and choose you. An aerial of your premises makes it easy to spot when they arrive.
