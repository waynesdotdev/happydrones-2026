The camera matters, but the room matters more. Twenty minutes of getting ready makes a bigger difference to your listing photos than any lens. Here's the checklist I send every seller before a shoot. You can also try the [interactive version](/property-photography/), where the room changes as you tick each item.

## Every room

- **Every light on**, even in daylight. Warm light makes rooms feel lived-in, not dark.
- **Curtains and blinds open**, so daylight comes in and buyers can see the view.
- **Surfaces cleared**: mugs, post, remotes and chargers out of sight.
- **Cushions and throws straight**: two minutes that make a room look looked-after.
- **Toys and pet beds away**: buyers want to picture their life, not yours.
- **Laundry and bins hidden**: airers, baskets and bins in a cupboard or the car.

## Kitchen

- Worktops cleared, except one or two nice things
- Fridge door cleared of magnets and notes
- Tea towels and bins away

## Bathroom

- Toilet seat down
- Bottles off the bath and shower
- Fresh towels, folded

## Bedrooms

- Beds made, pillows plumped
- Nothing on the floor
- Bedside tables cleared

## Outside (for the drone shots too)

- Cars off the drive if you can
- Bins out of sight
- Lawn mowed, toys and washing in

## On the day

You don't need to be in every room while I shoot. Most people make a cup of tea and stay out of the way, which is perfect: it keeps you out of the reflections.

If something's not right on the day, like a car you couldn't move, tell me. A lot can be framed around.
