Drone footage isn't just for estate agents. Here are five ways local businesses get value from a single shoot.

## 1. A website header that shows where you are

A slow aerial shot of your building and surroundings tells visitors what you look like and how to find you.

## 2. Short vertical clips for social media

Ten to fifteen seconds of something people haven't seen before is exactly what TikTok, Reels and Shorts reward.

## 3. An indoor tour before people visit

An FPV fly-through of a gym, shop, café or venue lets customers look round from their sofa.

## 4. Photos for your Google Business profile

Fresh, high-quality photos help your listing stand out next to competitors.

## 5. Progress and milestones

New extension, new car park, grand opening: aerial before-and-after shots make great announcements.
