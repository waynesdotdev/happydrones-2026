A lot has been added to Happy Drones this autumn. Here's a round-up, in case you missed any of it.

## New services

- **[Yearly roof health check](/services/roof-health-check/):** one check a year, with a reminder when it's due, from £119 a year.
- **[Storm and insurance claim pack](/services/storm-claim-pack/):** dated photos and a one-page summary the next working day, from £165.
- **[Gutter check](/services/gutter-check/):** a quick look into every gutter, from £79.
- **[Solar pre-survey pack](/services/solar-pre-survey/):** roof, layout and shading for installers, from £95.
- **[Before-and-after pack for trades](/services/before-after-pack/):** two visits, before and after, from £195.
- **[Construction time-lapse film](/services/construction-time-lapse/):** months of progress in one minute, from £149.
- **[Planning and design context photos](/services/planning-context/):** the site in its setting, from £195.
- **[Aerial 360° panoramas](/services/aerial-360/):** spin the view from above, from £95.
- **[Land and plot photos](/services/land-plot-photos/):** with the boundary drawn on, from £175.

## New on the website

- **[Weather check](/plan/):** see the forecast for your shoot date, the best time to fly and a better date if yours looks poor.
- **[Example roof report](/report/example/):** what you get after a roof check, as a web page you can share.
- **[Agent accounts](/estate-agents/#account):** no deposits, one monthly invoice.

## Coming soon

- **Thermal inspections:** heat loss, damp and solar panel checks
- **Mapping and 3D models:** measured maps and models of sites and roofs
- **Indoor fly-through tours:** one smooth flight through the home

None of these are bookable yet, but you can register your interest on each page and you'll hear first when they launch.
