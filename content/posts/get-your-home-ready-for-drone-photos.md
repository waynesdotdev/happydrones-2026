Buyers make up their minds in the first few photos. A little preparation before the shoot makes every room look bigger, brighter and easier to imagine living in.

## Outside

- Move cars off the drive and away from the front of the house
- Put bins out of sight, round the side or in the garage
- Mow the lawn a day or two before, so it has time to look fresh
- Tidy away hoses, toys and garden tools, and sweep the path

## Inside

- Open every curtain and blind, and switch on every light, including lamps
- Clear kitchen worktops apart from one or two nice items
- Hide toiletries, towels on the floor and the washing-up bowl
- Put the toilet seat down and straighten bedding and cushions
- Move pet bowls, beds and litter trays out of shot

> The best listing photos look effortless, but almost all of them started with someone moving the bins.

## On the day

Plan for 1–2 hours depending on the package. I'll start outside while you finish the last bits indoors, then work room by room. If we're filming a walkthrough or a glide tour, I'll walk the route with you first and agree which doors should be open.
